# 🎓 Education Umbrella

> Your trusted partner for a seamless international education journey.

**Education Umbrella** is a static website for an international education consultancy that helps students pursue their dreams of studying at universities worldwide.

The website provides information about university selection, application support, visa assistance, document services, online payments, and essential registrations such as NIF, NISS, and SNS.

---

## 🌍 About

Education Umbrella supports students throughout their study-abroad journey by providing personalized guidance and practical assistance.

Our services include:

- 🎓 University & course selection
- 📝 University application support
- 🛂 Visa assistance
- 📄 Document preparation & attestation
- 💳 Online payment support
- 🇵🇹 NIF registration
- 🇵🇹 NISS registration
- 🏥 SNS registration
- ✈️ Pre-departure guidance
- 🤝 Student consultation
- 🌍 International education opportunities
